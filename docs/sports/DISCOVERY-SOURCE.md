## Product thesis

Create a governed player-support workflow connecting scouting and acquisition evidence with the conditions a player needs to transition, develop and sustain performance. Start with a reviewable transition-and-development plan that club staff and the player can correct. Do not start with an unvalidated personality score or automatic buy/sell recommendation.

The lifecycle is: discover → review evidence → human acquisition decision → transition → support and development → review outcomes → loan/transfer continuity. Each stage shares selected evidence through permissions, not unrestricted access to everything known about a player.

## Initial demonstration

Use a clearly fictional adult player until a participant and data rights are confirmed. A scout imports a report; observations retain source, date and uncertainty. The club states the intended role and environment. The player contributes communication preferences, goals and transition concerns through akilii. A practitioner reviews a proposed 30-day transition plan with named action owners and review dates. Later check-ins show what was delivered and what the player found helpful.

Show gaps explicitly: a video observation is not a diagnosis, a coach's interpretation is not a player-confirmed fact, and a support hypothesis is not evidence of suitability for employment. Acquisition evidence and private player-care conversations require distinct access rules. Private wellbeing information must not silently enter recruitment rankings or valuation models.

## Commercial discovery

Confirm with Keriel and Roger: who is the actual buyer; who can authorise a pilot; which recurring decision needs improvement; which data is available and licensed; whether the player can participate; who owns support actions; and what the club already uses. Separate sourcing/transfer economics from assessment methodology and disclose conflicts. Do not claim independence solely because a report is generated by software.

A useful first commercial test is a paid, bounded design sprint followed by a supported transition case. Price and scope remain unagreed. No club, individual player, agent or multi-club group should be named publicly as a customer or partner on the basis of the recovered conversation.

## Evaluation and scope

Measure report-to-review time, evidence gaps resolved, plan actions completed, staff usefulness, player-perceived agency and repeated use. Compare against the club's existing workflow. Do not attribute sporting performance changes or transfer value to the product without a suitable study.

Begin with adult participants. Youth/academy deployment, medical interpretation, eligibility advice, biometric inference and consequential automated decisions require separate specialist design and review. No covert monitoring or inferred neurodivergence from footage, voice or appearance.
